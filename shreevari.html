ATELIER VALUES & METHODOLOGY
01

DESIGN-LED APPROACH

Prioritizing volumetric proportions, solar daylight orientation, and tectonic honesty over decorative and superficial cosmetic add-ons.

02

ONE TEAM, START TO FINISH

Seamless single-point accountability bridging municipal approvals, architectural drawings, engineering, and final joinery handover.

03

CLEAR TIMELINES & COSTS

Algorithmic project scheduling with milestone photographic audits, open-book material bills, and disciplined financial procurement.

04

LOCAL BENGALURU EXPERTISE

Deep mastery of Deccan geotechnical strata, BBMP sanction bylaws, local rainwater recharge methods, and regional quarry networks.

05

QUALITY MATERIALS & WORKMANSHIP

Low-embodied carbon masonry using hand-quarried Sadarahalli granite, unpolished lime plasters, fluted terracotta, and seasoned teak.

06

OPEN COMMUNICATION

Direct principal partner accessibility, 1:20 physical scale mockups on site, and structured weekly design-engineering reviews.

THE COURTYARD HOUSE

Conceived as a contemporary sanctuary responding to Bengaluru’s tropical savanna climate. By organizing living suites around a central open-sky water courtyard, the residence utilizes natural convective cooling currents to maintain comfort without mechanical cooling year-round.
