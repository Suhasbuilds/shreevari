5-stage engineering pipeline

  1. Stage one

    Site survey & feasibility

    Detailed plot evaluation, geotechnical Soil Bearing Capacity (SBC) testing, wind zone categorization, and structural load parameter formulation.

    Analysis
  2. Stage two

    3D structural modeling

    Precise STAAD.Pro structural modeling, member optimization, fabrication shop drawings, anchor bolt coordination, and municipal sanction plans.

    Design
  3. Stage three

    Plant fabrication

    Off-site automated CNC cutting, built-up I-beam welding, ultrasonic joint checks, shot blasting, and heavy-duty protective epoxy primer coating.

    Manufacturing
  4. Stage four

    Civil foundation works

    Concurrent site excavation, RCC footings casting, laser-calibrated anchor bolt installation, and heavy machine foundation execution.

    Substructure
  5. Stage five

    Erection & handover

    Rapid craned portal frame erection, insulated roof/wall Galvalume cladding, laser level torquing, interior fit-outs, and turnkey client handover.

    Completion ✓
